# MediScan
**MediScan** is an elderly-friendly voice and vision medicine strip reader built in **24 hours** for a hackathon. It helps older adults and patients identify medicine strips and understand what they are used for through large, high-contrast visuals and spoken audio in **English**, **हिंदी (Hindi)**, and **ਪੰਜਾਬੀ (Punjabi)**.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Multilingual Support (English, हिंदी, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ):** Switch UI labels, extracted summaries, and spoken voice output seamlessly across languages.
* **Image Upload & Camera Capture:** Upload a photo of a medicine strip or capture one directly using a camera.
* **Elderly-First Accessibility:** Large typography, clear visual hierarchy, high-contrast surfaces, and tactile controls designed for easy reading and tapping.
* **Spoken Audio Playback (Web Speech API):** Reads out the medicine name, manufacturer, strength, and basic purpose out loud—including a **"Not Sure"** safety prompt when an image is blurry or unclear.
* **Safety Guardrail ("Not Sure — Do Not Take Yet"):** Flags blurry, torn, or unclear strip readings so users are prompted to take a clearer photo or consult a family member or pharmacist.
